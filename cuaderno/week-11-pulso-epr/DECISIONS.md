# Decisions

## 2026-10-24 - Narrow the product to a commercial proof instrument

**Decision:** Build a cycle-effort and route comparator, not an EPR compliance platform.

**Why:** Recyda, Specright, and Lorax already cover packaging data and EPR workflows. The Blueprint assigns David the Money condition: prove recurring value and a payer before duplicating those capabilities.

## 2026-10-24 - Use aggregate sensing, not packaging files

**Decision:** Capture elapsed time, checkpoints, interruptions, and optional motion counts; collect no documents, identity, or free text.

**Why:** These signals test operational effort with a small attack surface. A document pipeline would exceed the weekly slice and trigger avoidable data and access risks.

## 2026-10-24 - Keep ML synthetic and bounded

**Decision:** Use a deterministic linear inference model with synthetic coefficients, a range, visible drivers, and no high-confidence state.

**Why:** No production dataset exists. A real-looking single prediction would be theater. The model earns continuation only by beating a simple measured-cycle rule on real second-cycle data.

## 2026-10-24 - Constraints are conjunctive

**Decision:** A route passes only if it meets cost, time, traceability, and data-control thresholds. No weighted score may compensate for a failed hard constraint.

**Why:** A blended score can hide a non-negotiable failure and manufacture a winner.

## 2026-10-24 - Preserve “none”

**Decision:** The app must be able to return no validated route.

**Why:** The Money lens should not force a build or purchase when the evidence does not justify one.

## 2026-10-24 - Human review is assumption review only

**Decision:** Require review before export while explicitly denying that the review is legal, environmental, or procurement approval.

**Why:** A button labeled approval can create compliance theater if its scope is unclear.

## 2026-10-24 - Remove runtime font dependency

**Decision:** Use system fonts rather than Google Fonts.

**Why:** The visual benefit did not justify a third-party request that contradicted the local-only privacy promise.

## 2026-10-24 - Fix persona-facing ambiguity

**Decision:** Repair projection-section contrast and rename the eligible verdict to “Pasa el filtro · no es recomendación.”

**Why:** Paula's synthetic walkthrough exposed a visual accessibility defect and the risk that green treatment could be read as a vendor recommendation.
